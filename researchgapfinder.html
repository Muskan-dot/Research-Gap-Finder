How to use

  1. Library: click a paper to see its limitations. Each limitation is a possible research gap.
  2. Save: drag a paper onto the dashed box to save it. Drop it again to remove it.
  3. Notes: inside a paper, write your own idea. It is stored in your browser.
  4. Add: add your own paper or project with its PDF and link, then open it.
  5. Gap Finder: type your idea. The score is the share of your keywords found in no paper. Open "the working" to see every step.

The score is plain keyword matching, so always check a gap against real papers.
